The science
Small-angle prediction
T ≈ 2π √(L / g)
T = period in seconds · L = string length in metres · g ≈ 9.81 m/s²
🌿
Longer string, longer period. The bob takes more time to complete one swing when the string is longer.
⚖
Mass is not the secret. In an ideal simple pendulum, changing bob mass does not change its period.
🍃
Real rooms are not perfect. Wind, vibration, damping and human reaction time can make a measured result differ from the ideal formula.
