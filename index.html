A Note from the Notebook

The Science
Small-Angle Theory
T ≈ 2π √(L / g)
T = period in seconds · L = string length in metres · g ≈ 9.81 m/s²
🌿
Longer string, longer period. The period T increases with the square root of length (T ∝ √L). Quadrupling string length doubles the period.
⚖
Mass is not the secret. In an ideal simple pendulum, gravitational force and inertia both scale directly with mass (F = mg = ma), so mass cancels out completely!
🍃
Real rooms are not perfect. Cross-breezes, mechanical bench vibrations, air resistance, and human reaction time create realistic experimental variance.
📐
Finite-angle correction. For larger angles (θ > 15°), the restoring force is non-linear (-g/L sin θ). The true period is slightly longer: T ≈ T₀(1 + 1/16 θ₀²).
